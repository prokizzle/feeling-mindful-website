# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Next.js 15 application with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion.

## Users

People looking for grounded tools for self-discovery, mindfulness, routines, and fitness. The primary website job is to introduce GoodParts and invite beta enquiries.

## Product Purpose

Feeling Mindful Labs is an independent app company. GoodParts is its flagship; Simple Rituals is complementary, and CardioEngine is a published fitness app.

## Positioning

The company builds reflective, human-centered apps. It does not offer consulting. PlayaOS and Waffles & House are free community software rooted in Burning Man and gifting, not revenue projects.

## Operating Context

Visitors discover GoodParts, then complementary apps and community work. The site works on desktop and mobile in light and dark themes. GoodParts and Simple Rituals are not publicly available. Beta access is requested by email, not a signup system or preorder.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing routes include the homepage, app detail pages, services, support, contact, privacy, terms, and data deletion.
- Existing content and functional links should remain truthful and usable.
- The shared navigation, footer, theme toggle, animations, and app data are reused across routes.
- GoodParts uses IFS-aware language and cloud transcription. Interface examples are explicitly labeled sample content, not screenshots.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Feeling Mindful Labs.
- Voice: quiet, contemplative, encouraging; never clinical or coach-y.
- The attached Feeling Mindful Design System is the source of truth for the family brand: warm paper and sandstone surfaces, forest ink, sage, terracotta/clay, DM Sans, and DM Serif Display.
- Avoid cold blue-purple gradients, neon, decorative glows, and glassy SaaS styling.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing logos, app icons, and warm editorial imagery in `public/images/` and `public/icons/`.
- Attached design system at `/Users/nicholasprokesch/Downloads/Feeling Mindful Design System.zip`.
- No testimonials, customer logos, or outcome metrics supplied; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Technology should support human work without taking over the room.
- Clarity and trust matter more than hype.
- Privacy and research grounding are meaningful product commitments.
- Each app can have its own character while belonging to one warm, coherent family.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts. The product audience includes people using mindfulness tools in quiet, low-stimulation contexts.
